# Kinetra — EcoDoor AI

Kinetra is a clean-technology venture developing EcoDoor AI, a smart door energy-harvesting and monitoring concept designed to promote energy efficiency and sustainable building management. The platform combines intelligent monitoring, energy analytics, interactive dashboards, and sustainability insights to explore how everyday door movement can contribute to smarter, greener spaces.

**Key Features**
- Smart door energy-harvesting concept
- Interactive energy monitoring dashboard
- Door activity and device status tracking
- Energy analytics and sustainability metrics
- Customer authentication and personalized dashboard
- Responsive, modern user interface
- Simulated data for demonstration and testing

**Vision:** Transform everyday movement into an opportunity for smarter energy use and a more sustainable future.

*Note: Energy generation and monitoring figures are simulated for demonstration unless connected to actual hardware.*
